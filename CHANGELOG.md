# Changelog

All notable changes to this project will be documented in this file.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/),
and this project adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [Unreleased]

### Changed
- The fight screen has a "Use an item" row between Reload and Run away, and `Fight::new` takes the item names. `Modifier::CritChance` gained `armor`, `Modifier::ALL_TAGS` is 22 and `Condition::ALL_TAGS` is 21; `ArmorClass` can be written. `Active.source` is a `BoostSource`. `QuestView` gained `minutes_since` (default `None`) and `Combatant` gained `crit_chance_against` (default 0), so a state or fighter written before them still compiles. `Game::sell` and `sell_copy` price with the vendor (`sell_price_to`), which is the old price unless the vendor deals flat.
- The save is now version 7: `rewards` lists the rewards for close fights still in force and `quest_times` the minute of each quest moment. A version 6 save is upgraded on reading with neither, and a save that names an unknown quest or stage, a moment that has not happened yet, a repeated moment or a worn-off reward is refused.
- The usable-consumables test expects 170 (it was 167); the wiki-number test reads the auto-inject super stimpak ("+72-180") and the snakebite tourniquet from the text, like Rad-X; the perk list test names Light Touch and Just Lucky I'm Alive; the fight-row test counts Reload fourth from the end.
- `Combat` gained `replace_combatant` (a new `CombatError::MaxHpChanged`) and `computer_turns_then`, which calls a hook after each computer attack; `computer_turns` is the same with no hook. `Battle` keeps a `PlayerKit` and rebuilds the player's fighter when their health moves into another situation, so a perk that reads health follows it round by round. `Arsenal::player` became the crate-private `player_kit`.
- `DerivedStat` gained `DamageThreshold` and `DamagePercent` (the item schema's stat list too), and `BoostTally` gained `damage_threshold` and `damage_percent`; a fight adds them to the player's threshold and weapon damage. Slasher's record in `data/items/uses.json` gained its +25% damage, which the wiki lists beside the resistance it already gave.
- The barter formulas in `data/rules/formulas.json` cite the wiki's own page and no longer call the 80-point row of 81 a typo; their numbers are unchanged. Chet gives no price cut now (the wiki says his prices follow Barter alone), so `only_chet_and_trudy_give_a_price_cut` is `only_trudy_gives_a_price_cut` and the TUI shop test that showed his cut uses Trudy's shop.
- A rule that reads the player's standing is checked when the standing changes: `Condition::reads_standing`, `QuestBook::standing_quests`, and `Game` runs the change and those rules as one unit. The golden scenario for the Goodsprings gunfight lock now has the lock land with the infamy, so the later `start_quest` is refused. `normalize.py` can tell two rules with the same kind and target apart by the wiki's `when` text.
- `Game` has no effect it refuses as "not built" any more: `companion_join` and `companion_leave` work, and the `not_built` helper is gone. The three tests that used a companion effect as their unbuilt feature check a stage reward in caps and a companion effect for nobody instead. `CompanionView` is implemented for `Game` from the party.
- The save is now version 6: `party` lists the companions with the player. A version 5 save is upgraded on reading with nobody in the party, and a save that names a companion the game lacks, repeats one or lists more than two is refused.
- `GameData` gained `companions`, and a scoped load keeps the NPC of every companion so they can fight wherever the player is. `Character` gained `set_company`, the modifiers the party's perks give. `better_healing` carries `heal_percent` 20, so the test that lists the perks with modifiers names it.
- `Condition` gained `TimeOfDay` (`ALL_TAGS` is 20), `WorldView` gained `minute_of_day` with a default of 08:00, and `Modifier` gained `Special` and `AllSpecial` (`ALL_TAGS` is 20). `Perk` and `Trait` gained `conditional`, and `Character` gained `situation` and `set_situation`; a character built without them behaves as before.
- The three Ghost Town Gunfight tests that listed the allies now expect the two settlers that come with Trudy.
- The radiation check over uses also reads "Rads", the spelling some of the new items use.
- The save is now version 5: it holds `poison` and each carried weapon's `mods`. A version 4 save is upgraded on reading with no poison and no mods fitted, and a save that fits a mod to a weapon it does not suit, or lists one twice, is refused.
- `Arsenal` reads a weapon's fitted mods when it builds the fighter and when it works out how much wear the weapon can take, so a mod that raises the condition raises it everywhere. `BoostStat` and `DerivedStat` gained `PoisonResistance`.
- `Modifier` can no longer be copied (a modifier can now name an item), and `Bonuses` keeps the fight and healing modifiers whole. `Combatant` gained `kind`, `against` and `ignored_threshold`, all with defaults, so a fighter written before them still compiles.
- A stimpak now restores 30 hit points and one percent more for every two points of Medicine (a stand-in: the wiki says Medicine changes how well stimpaks work and gives no formula). Its record in `data/items/uses.json` is `medicine_heal` instead of `heal`, and `ItemUse` gained `MedicineHeal`.
- `Unavailable::Breakdown` is gone: the sixteen breakdown recipes are sorted like the others, and the four that name no item to take apart report `Unavailable::NoSubject`. `Formula` gained `work`, `tools` and `outputs`, and `Crafted` gained `parts`; `output` still gives the first thing a recipe gives.
- The automatic save now keeps the one before it. `SaveFolder::write_automatic` writes it, and the game's autosave calls it. `Slot` gained `Previous` and `Legacy`, and `SaveName` refuses `autosave_previous` as well as `autosave`.
- `Command` gained `Delete` (d or Delete) and `Rename` (r); only the saves screen uses them, and the other screens ignore them.
- The saves folder in the TUI tests now sits inside a folder of the test's own, so the old save beside it is the test's too.
- Weapons are carried one by one. `Inventory::stacks` now lists only non-weapon items; `Inventory::weapons` lists each weapon with its `CopyId` and the condition points it has lost, and `item_count`, `weight`, `give_item`, `take_item`, `discard` and `equip` treat weapons as before in total (taking weapons away removes the most worn spare first and the one in hand last; `equip` takes the least worn copy). `Game::weapon_condition(&ItemId)` is replaced by `Game::copy_condition(CopyId)`, `Game::repair_with_kit`, `repair_quote` and `repair_at` take the weapon's `CopyId`, `Game::merge_weapons(keep, gone)` merges two named copies, and `Game::worn_weapons` returns `WornWeapon` records. The pack, trade and repair screens list each weapon as its own row with its condition, and the pack offers "Merge another copy into this one" with a choice of copy.
- The save is now version 3: `pack.weapons` lists each carried weapon with its number and wear, `pack.wielded` names the weapon in hand, and the per-kind `wear` list is gone. A version 2 save is upgraded on reading: the wear of a kind becomes the wear of each copy of that kind.
- Saves are now a folder of named saves (`<name>.json`) beside an automatic save, not one file. The default folder is `fnv/saves/` in the user's data folder; the old single `save.json` is listed as "Old save" for loading only (see Added). "Save game" and "Load game" open a list; saving offers "New save..." (type a name) and every named save to write over (after asking); loading offers every save, the automatic one included.
- Fights start in turn order: the fighter with the highest Sequence acts first, equal Sequences keep the order given. `Battle::open` plays any enemy turns that come before the player's first one, and the combat screen calls it when a fight starts. The "Back" key in a fight now points at "Run away".
- `Status` gained `Fled` and `Outcome` gained `Fled`, so code that matches on either must handle a fight the player escaped.
- A vendor record may carry `restock_days`; the vendor schema gained it. `Game::wait` (and so sleep and travel) refills the shops on restock days.
- Weapon wear can now be undone: `Game::weapon_condition` is joined by `Game::repair_with_kit`, `Game::merge_weapons` and `Game::repair_at`, and the pack screen offers "Mend with a kit" and "Merge two copies" on a worn weapon.
- `ItemBook` now holds crafting components and other miscellany (123 more items, with the new `ItemClass::Misc`), so scrap, tape and tools can be carried, sold and used up by recipes; the pack-screen names include them.
- `Stage` gained `optional`: reaching an optional stage when the quest is already past it changes nothing instead of being refused, so side tasks can be done in any order. Stages 30 and 40 of Run Goodsprings Run are optional.
- Chet's shakedown (Barter 25 or Speech 25) now moves Run Goodsprings Run to stage 30, and Doc Mitchell's Medicine 25 lie moves it to stage 40, in either order.
- `recipes.json` records a trailing "(1)" footnote on an ingredient name as the wiki's marker, not part of the name, so the weapon repair kit's wrench now resolves to an item. The recipes also carry `station` as one of three kinds and an optional `on_craft`.
- The save is now version 2 (it gained `gathered`); a version 1 save is upgraded on reading with nothing gathered.
- The scene gained rows for finds, crafting stations and repairers, between the beds and "Character". The scene tests that count rows or want every row on screen use a taller terminal (50 rows) or count from the new positions.

- The player's chance to hit, and the foes', now comes from skill and weapon spread instead of a flat 65%: `40 + skill / 2 - 3 * spread`, kept between 5 and 95 (a stand-in curve). With the starting pistol (Guns 15, spread 0.7) the chance is 45%. `Battle` fights over `Combat<Fighter, AimedHitChance>`; `FlatHitChance` stays for tests and benchmarks.
- A reload now costs the weapon's own reload time as action points (one shot per second of reload), capped at the fighter's whole turn, instead of a flat 2.
- The damage multiplier for weapon condition now follows the wear on the player's weapon (full when new, half when ruined); a foe's weapon never wears.
- The scene gained "Save game" and "Load game" before "Quit game"; the scene tests count rows from the new end, and the first-frame test uses a taller terminal so the longer list still shows "Quit game" without scrolling.
- `BoostStat` and `DerivedStat` can now be written as well as read, so a boost in force can be saved.
- `ItemUse` can no longer be copied (a boost item carries a list), so `ItemBook::item_use` and `Inventory::item_use` return a clone; `Game::use_item` now also starts boosts, and an item that heals is still refused at full health while one that only boosts is always used.
- `Character` reads the boosts in force: attributes, skills, maximum hit points, action points and carry weight all include them, and the earned attributes and skills are never touched, so a boost that ends puts back exactly what was there. Level-up skill points still use the earned Intelligence.
- `Game::wait` ends the boosts whose time has come; `Game::sleep` and `Game::travel` pass time through it. When a boost to maximum hit points ends the wounds stay, so the extra hit points go with it, and a player who was alive keeps at least one.
- The Ghost Town Gunfight now has up to six attackers, as the wiki says: Joe Cobb and two Powder Gangers always, and three more unless Trudy joined the militia, Easy Pete joined, or Chet supplied it. A fully prepared town faces the old three.
- `Game::buy` charges the vendor's price (`Game::buy_price_from`), which includes the vendor's cut when the player has earned one. `Game::buy_price` still gives the list price.
- The scene's rows after "Pack" are now "Journal", "Reputation" and "Travel", then the waits and "Quit game".
- Healing powder is now a usable item (it was unlisted while its Perception penalty was not carried).
- `Game` applies reputation and karma effects instead of refusing them as unbuilt, so a quest reward that pays fame, infamy or karma now takes effect. A faction that tracks no reputation is refused. The Ghost Town Gunfight gate reads the wiki's rule exactly: Ringo stops offering the quest at infamy tier 3 with Goodsprings, whatever the fame (it was "Shunned or worse", which missed players with some fame).
- The tests that used karma as their example of an unbuilt feature (`tests/game/state.rs`, `tests/game/automation.rs`) now use companions, which are still unbuilt.

- The save is now version 4: it gained `rads` and `addictions`. A version 3 save is upgraded on reading with no radiation and no addictions.
- `Combat` gained a lead (`Combat::with_lead`, `Combat::lead`, `CombatError::LeadOnTheEnemySide`): when one is named, the player's side loses when the lead falls, not when every fighter on it has. `Battle::open` and `Battle::end_turn` now play the allies as well as the enemies (`computer_turns`); `enemy_turns` is unchanged. `Encounter` gained `allies`.
- In the Ghost Town Gunfight, Easy Pete and Trudy no longer take a ganger out of the fight when they joined the militia: they fight on the player's side, and the foes are the wiki's six unless Chet's supplies thin them to five. Sunny always fights beside the player.
- The automatic save keeps five earlier ones, not one. `Slot` gained `Earlier(Depth)`, and `SaveName` refuses `autosave_previous_2` onwards as well. Opening the saves screen moves the old `save.json` into the folder as the named save "Old save"; `Slot::Legacy` is still read and deleted as before.
- `Condition`, `Effect`, `CharacterView` and `CharacterSink` gained radiation and addiction: `Condition::Radiation`, `Condition::Addicted`, `Effect::Cure` (with `Ailment`), `CharacterView::{radiation, addicted}` (defaults: none) and `CharacterSink::cure` (no default, so every sink must say what a cure does). The `BoostUse` of an item gained `rads`, and a boost can change `radiation_resistance`.
- `Game::use_item` still never rolls for addiction; the pack screen now calls `Game::use_item_rolling`, which returns what the dose did (`Used`). `InventoryError` gained `NotIrradiated`.
- The status line shows the rads and the addictions when there are any. The combat screen lists the allies on one line and scrolls its actions when they do not fit.
- Three tests that assumed the old stand-ins changed: the number of breakdowns that can be done (12 to 15), the number of usable consumables (16 to 94), and the gunfight's attackers (the helpers' effect). Four TUI tests that read the fight screen at 30 rows now read it at 40, and the reload test finds the Reload row instead of counting foes.

### Fixed
- Nerd Rage and every other perk that reads health now follows the player's hit points inside a fight; before, a fight read them once when it opened.
- A fight no longer wears every copy of the wielded weapon's kind: only the weapon fought with wears, and a spare bought later is new.
- The scene's action list now scrolls with the cursor when it is taller than the scene, instead of cutting off the last rows (including "Quit game") at small terminal sizes.

### Added

The item, the pack and the fight change together or not at all; a chem works from the next shot. A benchmark stub, `benches/fight_items.rs`.
- Items that work by themselves: `AutoUse`, `Consumable.auto_use`, `ItemBook::{with_autos, auto_use}`, `Game::{auto_use, auto_use_in_battle}`, `scripts/normalize.py` reading `auto` from `data/items/uses.json`, and the schema entry. The auto-inject stimpak, the auto-inject super stimpak and the snakebite tourniquet are usable (170 of 195).
- Light Touch and Just Lucky I'm Alive in `data/perks/modifiers.json`, with `Modifier::{CritChanceAgainst, AfterFightBoost}`, `armor` on `CritChance`, `Character::{edge_in, after_fight_rewards}`, `Combatant::crit_chance_against` and `BoostSource`.

A companion in the party fights beside the player in every fight, and Arcade Gannon grants Better Healing while with the player. A benchmark stub, `benches/companions.rs`.

- Time of day: `MinuteOfDay` (written `"HH:MM"`), `TimeWindow` (crosses midnight; equal ends refused), the `time_of_day` condition and `common.schema.json` entries. Property tests: a window and its swap split the day; the start is inside and the end is out.
- Conditional perks and traits: `ConditionalModifiers` (a `when` list and the modifiers it switches on, limited to health and the hour, refused when it could change what it reads), `Situation`, `Game::settle`, `special` and `all_special` modifiers, and a `conditional` list in `data/perks/modifiers.json` for Nerd Rage, Night Person, Hot Blooded and Early Bird. Tests on hand-built and real characters, a path-independence property test, and a benchmark stub (about 38 ns per situation change).

- Poison: `poison/` (`Poison`, `taken_in`, `DRAIN_PER_MINUTE`), `Game::{poison, poison_resistance, with_poison}`, the `Ailment::Poison` cure, and the `poison_resistance` boost stat. Time turns poison into wounds at 5 points a minute (a stand-in), and poison alone never takes the last hit point (a stand-in: the wiki lets it kill). `Used` gained `poison_in` and `cured_poison`; `InventoryError` gained `NotPoisoned`, and an antidote is kept by a player who is not poisoned.
- Five more usable consumables in `data/items/uses.json`: antivenom, datura antivenom, fiery purgative, blood shield and bleak venom. A boost may now carry `poison` and `cure_poison`; `item.schema.json` and `common.schema.json` say so.
- Weapon mods: `weapon_mods/` (`WeaponModBook`, `Fitting`, `FitError`, `WeaponModError`), `Game::{fit_mod, remove_mod, mods_for, fitted_mods, fitting, with_weapon_mods}` and `data/engine/weapon_mods.json` (111 mods, 59 with effects `normalize.py` reads from the wiki text). A fitted mod adds damage, adds rounds to the magazine, narrows spread and raises the condition a weapon has when new; a mod the game cannot act on (a silencer, a scope) fits and changes nothing. Taking a mod off leaves a weapon at its new, lower limit if it was more worn than that.
- The pack screen offers "Fit a mod" and "Take a mod off" on a weapon (only when a fitting mod is carried or one is fitted) and lists the mods fitted; the status line shows poison beside rads.
- Tests: `tests/poison/` (laws and property tests), `tests/weapon_mods/` (real data and fitting through `Game`), `tests/game/poison.rs` and two pack-screen tests.
- Allies in combat. An encounter lists `allies` (a fighter record like a foe, present while its `when` conditions hold); they act in turn order and play themselves, shooting the first enemy standing. `combat::computer_turns` plays everyone but the player; schema, validator and the Rust types follow, and a property test shows nobody shoots their own side, the turn always comes back to the player, and the status follows the player's fall.
- Radiation: `radiation/` (`Radiation`, `absorbed`, `RADS_PER_TIER`), the bounded `Rads`, `Game::{rads, radiation_tier, radiation_resistance, with_rads}`, and sickness (one Endurance and one Agility per 200 rads) as a tally the character reads. A property test shows the rads stay in range, resistance never lets more in, and a sicker player is never better off.
- Addiction: `addiction/` (`AddictionBook`, `AddictionKind`, `Addictions`, `WITHDRAWAL_MINUTES`), `Game::{addictions, addiction_risk, with_addiction, with_addictions}` and withdrawal (one Strength and one Agility per addiction, over after three days without a dose). A property test shows 0% never and 100% always addicts, a dose while addicted starts nothing new, and nothing held has gone three days without a dose after expiry.
- 78 more usable consumables in `data/items/uses.json`: 75 meals and drinks, RadAway, Rad-X and the super stimpak. `normalize.py` resolves the .357 Magnum, .44 Magnum and 20 gauge breakdowns to their one plain round.
- Doc Mitchell's cures: "Can you heal my radiation?" (100 caps, from the transcript) and "I think I might be addicted to something" (free: the transcript names no price), each shown only to a player who needs it.
- Two benchmark stubs (`radiation`, `addiction`) and a seven-scenario golden set for the cures; the scenario runner can now start a player irradiated or addicted.
- Conditional perk and trait effects. `Modifier` gained eleven kinds: `damage_threshold`, `damage_resistance`, `ignore_threshold`, `damage_percent`, `crit_chance`, `crit_damage_percent`, `ap_cost_percent`, `reload_ap_percent`, `spread_percent`, `weapon_wear_percent` and `heal_percent`. Six of them can be limited to a weapon skill (`skill`), to a kind of foe (`foe`) or to one item (`item`), and apply only then. `common.schema.json` gained them and a `foe_kind` set, and the contract tests keep the two sides in step.
- `FoeKind` (human, robot, animal, mutated animal, mutated insect, super mutant, abomination), and an optional `kind` on each foe of an encounter (a person when absent).
- `Character::edge` (what the perks and traits change in a fight with the weapon in hand), `Character::wear_percent` and `Character::heal_percent`; `Fighter` builds its threshold, resistance, critical chance, damage, action point costs and spread from them, and `Combat` reads the bonus against the foe's kind and the threshold ignored. `Game::finish_battle` wears the weapon by the character's wear percent, and `Game::use_item` heals by the character's heal percent.
- Ten more perks and five more traits are modeled in `data/perks/modifiers.json`: Toughness, Finesse, Better Criticals, Math Wrath, Rapid Reload, Piercing Strike, Fast Metabolism, Demolition Expert, Entomologist and Hunter; Built to Destroy, Kamikaze, Heavy Handed, Fast Shot and Trigger Discipline. Perks and traits whose text has a part the game cannot carry yet stay unmodeled, and the picker says so.
- Taking things apart. A recipe in `breakdown` mode spends the item it names (as many as `output_qty`, one when absent) and gives the listed parts, at the station it names; twelve of the sixteen can be done at the Goodsprings reloading bench, and a test checks that a round taken apart never gives back more than making it used. The craft screen lists them as "Take apart ..." with what they give.
- Tools that are not used up. `Ingredient.kept` marks a tool the wiki calls "not expended"; `normalize.py` reads "(2, not expended)" as two carried tools. A recipe needs them in the pack and leaves them there. Only two recipes carry the mark, and both stay unmade for other reasons.
- Save management on the saves screen: `d` deletes a save and `r` renames a named one, each after a question, and a rename never replaces another save. `SaveFolder::delete` and `SaveFolder::rename` do the work.
- The previous automatic save (`autosave_previous.json`), listed and loadable as "Previous autosave". An unreadable autosave is never kept over a good previous one.
- The old single `save.json`, one level above the saves folder, is listed as "Old save" and can be loaded or deleted, but never written.
- Benchmark stub for the fight edge read in `benches/character.rs` (about 17 ns).
- Weapon instances in `crates/engine/src/inventory/copies.rs`: `CopyId` (never reused within a pack) and `WeaponCopy`, with `Inventory::{weapons, weapon, copies_of, wielded, equip_copy, set_lost, discard_copy, merge_copies, restore_weapon}`. Merging keeps the chosen copy, removes the other, and hands the hand to the survivor if the merged-away copy was in hand. `Game::{equip_copy, drop_copy, sell_copy}`: a weapon is equipped, thrown away or sold as the exact copy chosen, and a sold weapon joins the vendor's stock as a plain new item. Property test against a model of (number, wear) pairs: wear stays with the copy it was written to through any sequence of gives, takes, discards, equips and wear.
- Restocking in `crates/engine/src/trade/`: on every multiple of a vendor's interval (3 days unless `restock_days` says) since the game began, the vendor goes back to the stock and caps of the book; what the player sold them is gone with the refill. The shops depend on the clock alone, so waiting in one step or many gives the same shops (a property test). `Market::restock(from, to)` returns the vendors refilled.
- Initiative and fleeing in `crates/engine/src/combat/`: `Combatant::sequence` (higher acts first; the player's is twice Perception, a stand-in) and `Combatant::flee_chance`; `Combat::flee` is one roll against the runner's chance, only the player's side can try, escaping ends the fight with `Status::Fled`, and a failed try costs the rest of the turn. The game's flee chance is `25 + 6 * Agility` kept between 5 and 95 (a stand-in: the wiki gives no formula). `Battle::flee` plays the foes' turns after a failed run. A fled fight settles with `Outcome::Fled`: the wounds taken, the rounds fired and the wear on the weapon stay, nothing is won and the encounter stays on offer. The combat screen has "Run away (N% to get out)".
- Named saves in `crates/engine/src/saves/folder.rs`: `SaveName` (letters, digits, spaces, `-` and `_`, up to 40 characters, never `autosave`), `Slot`, `SaveFolder` (`write`, `read`, `list`, newest first) and `SaveSummary` (day, time, place and experience read without loading the game). A file that is not a save is listed with its reason; files with other names are left alone. A property test shows an accepted name always stays inside the folder.
- Autosave: after a walk, after a sleep and after a fight is settled while the player is alive, the game writes its own slot (`autosave.json`), which never overwrites a named save. A failed autosave is said in the log and does not stop the game. It is switched on by `App::with_autosave` from `main`, so tests never write to the player's folder.
- Typing a save name: while a name is being typed the front end hands raw keys to `App::type_key` instead of reading them as commands (so `q`, `j` and digits are letters); Ctrl+C still quits.
- Repair in `crates/engine/src/repair/`: the laws as pure arithmetic over condition points. A kit lifts a weapon to the Repair skill's share of its condition (rounded up, never worse); merging two copies adds a 10% bonus on top of what is left of both, held to the same ceiling and never below the better copy; a vendor charges up to half the weapon's value for a full repair, rounded up to at least one cap. Every rate is a stand-in: the wiki gives none. `Game::repair_with_kit` uses up a weapon repair kit, `Game::merge_weapons` uses up a spare copy (both copies share one wear record, so the pair is as worn as the record says), and `Game::repair_quote` and `Game::repair_at` have a vendor mend a weapon for caps, up to the vendor's own Repair skill, moving caps between the player and the vendor together or not at all. A vendor record may carry `repair` (Chet, Repair 50, a placeholder). - Twelve JSON Schemas in `schemas/`, including the shared `condition` / `effect` vocabulary
- `scripts/normalize.py`, which turns the raw data into engine-ready data in `data/engine/` with stable ids and cross-references, and a derived travel graph over the map markers
- `scripts/validate.py`, which validates every record against its schema, checks references between files, and self-tests the formulas against the wiki's worked examples
- Cargo workspace on edition 2024 with a headless `engine` library and a `tui` binary (`fnv-terminal`), organised by game capability
- Workspace lints: `unsafe` forbidden, `unwrap` denied outside tests
- `scripts/check`: one command that regenerates and validates the data, then runs rustfmt, clippy and the tests
- `ROADMAP.md` and a decision log in `DIARY.md`
- Typed data model in `crates/engine/src/shared/`: distinct id types, bounded skill and S.P.E.C.I.A.L. levels, a non-empty list, a `Redacted` log wrapper, and the `Condition` and `Effect` enums, with unit tests, property tests and two-way contract tests against `schemas/common.schema.json`
- Data loader in `crates/engine/src/shared/`: typed records for items, quests, NPCs, dialogue, factions and locations, and a `GameData` loader with a `Scope` (everywhere, or around one location such as Goodsprings). Errors name the file and the record id; duplicate ids and broken dialogue topic links are rejected
- New id types `LocationId`, `DialogueId`, `TopicKey` and `AmmoFamilyId`
- Loader tests against the real data and against deliberately broken data sets, and a load benchmark
- Condition/effect evaluator in `crates/engine/src/shared/`: `evaluate` for conditions, `apply` and `apply_all` for effects, six read-only view traits with default answers (`QuestView`, `StandingView`, `CharacterView`, `InventoryView`, `CompanionView`, `WorldView`), six write-side sink traits, `EmptyWorld`, `EffectError` and `BatchError`
- `apply_all` rejects a batch that overspends items or caps, or carries a NaN or infinite amount, before changing anything
- `Default` for `SkillLevel` and `SpecialLevel` (the lowest allowed value)
- Evaluator tests: examples for each condition, property tests for the laws of `all`/`any`/`not`, comparison ordering, effect round trips and batch behaviour, and an evaluation benchmark stub
- Quest engine in `crates/engine/src/quests/`: `QuestBook` (validated quest stages and rules) and `QuestLog` (player progress implementing `QuestView` and `QuestSink`). Quests go `not started -> active -> completed | failed`, finished quests never reopen, stages only move forward, and a `finish` stage completes the quest
- Quest tests: lifecycle cases, property tests over random effect sequences, a walk of every staged quest in the real data, and a playthrough benchmark stub
- Game state in `crates/engine/src/game/`: `Game` holds the quest log and implements all six view and six sink traits, so `evaluate` and `apply_all` run against it. Views of unbuilt features give the shared defaults; effects aimed at them are refused with a reason naming the missing feature
- Game tests: quest effects and conditions through `Game`, a property test for the refusals, a walk of every staged quest through `apply_all`, and a batch benchmark stub
- World state in `crates/engine/src/world/`: `WorldBook` (the NPC roster) and `WorldState` (story flags, NPC fates, ending locks) implementing `WorldView` and `WorldSink`. An NPC is alive (hostile or not), dead or removed, so a dead hostile NPC cannot exist; an NPC outside the roster is an error
- `Game::with_world` builds a game over a populated NPC roster, and `Game::meet` records meeting an NPC
- World tests: one case per rule, property tests over random effect sequences, world effects through `Game`, and a benchmark stub
- Goodsprings quest slice: `data/quests/slice_goodsprings.json` (hand-curated stage rewards and two rule gates) merged into `data/engine/quests.json` by `normalize.py`; stages gain an optional `on_enter` effect list in `quest.schema.json`
- `Game` applies a stage's `on_enter` effects the first time the stage is reached and fires a quest's consequence rules, once each, when the quest changes; a change that fails is undone as a whole
- `Game::deferred()` and `EffectError::NotBuilt`: rewards for unbuilt features (caps, XP, items) are kept instead of lost
- `validate.py` checks the quests, factions, items and NPCs named in authored conditions and effects
- Language-neutral golden scenarios in `tests/scenarios/goodsprings.json`, automation tests with a property test, and a plain-versus-automated benchmark
- Dialogue runner in `crates/engine/src/dialogue/`: `DialogueBook` (topic graph checked once: unique ids and keys, links resolve, an ending has no follow-ups) and `Conversation` (opens at the start topics; a topic with no follow-ups returns there; only topics whose conditions hold are offered; a failing `check` lists the option but locks it; a topic's effects apply as a unit or not at all; an ending topic closes the talk). It runs over any `State + Clone`, so it does not import `game`
- `start` (dialogue) and `ends` (topic) fields in `dialogue.schema.json`, the Rust records and the loader, which rejects a `start` link to a missing topic
- Goodsprings dialogue slice: `data/dialogue/slice_goodsprings.json` (hand-authored start menus, follow-ups, conditions and quest effects for Doc Mitchell and Sunny Smiles) merged into `data/engine/dialogue/` by `normalize.py`; `validate.py` checks its links and the quests and NPCs it names
- Dialogue tests: one case per rule, book rejection cases, a property test over random graphs and walks, the real data, a reachability check for authored topics, seven golden scenarios in `tests/scenarios/goodsprings_dialogue.json`, and a menu benchmark stub
- Combat core in `crates/engine/src/combat/`: `Combat` (a turn-based fight between two sides with action points, rounds and a win check), the `Combatant` trait (a fighter's numbers, read through a small trait), the damage pipeline from `formulas.json` (weapon damage, then resistance capped at 85, then threshold with a 20% floor), and bounded `Points`, `Multiplier` and `Percent` types. Hit chance sits behind the `HitModel` trait with a flat stand-in, and chance is rolled through a `Dice` trait, so a fight is deterministic for a given sequence of rolls
- Combat tests: one case per rule, property tests (armor never adds damage or blocks a fifth, more armor never lets more through, random play keeps the fight consistent, a fight of sure hits always ends), twelve golden scenarios in `tests/scenarios/combat.json`, a check of the rules against `formulas.json`, and a fight benchmark stub
- TUI v0 in `crates/tui/` (`fnv-terminal`, built on `ratatui`): a persistent layout (status line, scene, log, active-quest panel, key footer), an exploration screen listing who to talk to in Goodsprings, and a dialogue screen that drives the engine's `Conversation`. A new game starts the opening quest; locked topics are shown dimmed. Run it from the project root with `cargo run -p tui`
- TUI shared pieces: key-to-command mapping, a bounded menu cursor, a word-wrapped log, the layout split (with a "terminal too small" message below 70x22) and a Pip-Boy green theme
- TUI tests: property tests for the menu, the log and the layout, and headless `App` tests over the real Goodsprings data using ratatui's `TestBackend`

### Changed
- `Names` and `Quantity` moved from `crates/tui/src/trade/` to `crates/tui/src/shared/`, because the pack screen uses them too; the trade screen behaves as before.
- The scene's row order is now people, shops, fights, Character, Pack, Quit.
- Doc's starting kit (`ain_t_that_a_kick_in_the_head`, stage 50) also gives 30 rounds of 9mm, since the pistol cannot shoot without them; `tests/scenarios/goodsprings.json` states them
- The test helper that plays a fight reloads when the weapon runs empty
- The Ghost Town Gunfight is offered at stage 65 ("Join up with Sunny") instead of stage 10, so it no longer opens as soon as Sunny agrees; the optional stages 20 to 50 are skipped until Trudy, Chet, Doc and Easy Pete are authored
- `GameData` also loads `encounters.json`, and a location-scoped load keeps the NPC records of the foes of encounters at that location. The exploration screen offers the fights on offer after the people; the player starts in leather armor and takes the 9mm pistol in hand when a fight starts (stand-ins until there is an inventory screen)
- XP, caps and item rewards now apply to the game instead of being postponed into `Game::deferred()`, which keeps only rewards for unbuilt features (reputation, karma, companions). A reward that names an item outside the item book fails and the whole change is undone
- `tests/scenarios/goodsprings.json` states the caps, XP and items each scenario must leave behind instead of listing deferred effects
- `Game` now applies flag, NPC and ending effects instead of refusing them; `Game::new` has an empty roster, so NPC effects on it fail with an unknown-NPC error
- Effects aimed at features that are not built now fail with `EffectError::NotBuilt` instead of `Refused`
- A quest condition must now carry a `stage` exactly when its state is `stage_at_least` or `stage_is`, and stage numbers cannot be negative (`schemas/common.schema.json`)
