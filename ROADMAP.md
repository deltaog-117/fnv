# Roadmap

This document outlines the future direction of fnv-terminal-ai-game, a
turn-based terminal game inspired by Fallout: New Vegas, written in Rust.
Items are organized by priority, not by timeline.

---

## ✅ Completed (Milestones Achieved)

- ✅ Game data imported from the Fallout Wiki into raw JSON: 1,182 items (weapons, armor, ammo, consumables, weapon mods, skill books, crafting components), 256 quests, 880 NPCs, 517 dialogue transcripts, 684 locations with map positions, 201 perks, 16 traits, 42 factions and towns, 31 creatures, stats, levels, challenges, recipes and ending slides
- ✅ 116 hand-curated quest consequence rules (fails, locks, unlocks, exclusive paths)
- ✅ 13 JSON Schemas, including the shared `condition` / `effect` vocabulary
- ✅ Normalizer and validator (Python tooling): stable ids, cross-references between files, and formula self-tests
- ✅ Hand-curated engine formulas (skills, HP, AP, carry weight, resistances, XP, DR/DT damage) cross-checked against the wiki's own examples
- ✅ Derived travel graph over the 1024x1024 map markers
- ✅ Stack decision: Rust with `ratatui` for the game, Python for data tooling only
- ✅ Cargo workspace scaffold: a headless `engine` library and a thin `tui` binary, with one module per game capability, shared contracts, and a composition root
- ✅ `scripts/check`: regenerates and validates the data, then runs rustfmt, clippy and the tests, printing output only on failure
- ✅ Decision log (`DIARY.md`) covering the language, the contracts-based architecture, the data sources and the quality gate
- ✅ Typed data model in `crates/engine/src/shared/`: distinct id types, bounded numbers, the `Condition` and `Effect` vocabulary, and contract tests (property tests plus two-way schema checks) that fail when the Rust types and `common.schema.json` drift apart
- ✅ Data loader (`shared/load.rs`): reads `data/engine/` into typed catalogs, scoped to everywhere or to a location such as Goodsprings, with errors that name the file and record id; a load benchmark (about 25 ms for Goodsprings, 49 ms for everything)

- ✅ Condition/effect evaluator (`shared/eval.rs`): `evaluate` over six small read-only view traits with default answers, `apply` over six write-side sink traits (exhaustive, so a new `Effect` variant does not compile until handled), and `apply_all`, which checks a whole list against the state before applying any of it; law, round-trip and batch property tests, and an evaluation benchmark stub
- ✅ Quest engine (`quests/`): `QuestBook` validates quest stages and rules once; `QuestLog` implements `QuestView` and `QuestSink` with terminal completed/failed, forward-only stages and finish-stage completion; lifecycle, property and real-data tests, and a playthrough benchmark stub
- ✅ Game state (`game/`): `Game` holds the quest log and implements every view and sink trait; unbuilt features answer reads with defaults and refuse writes, so `evaluate` and `apply_all` already run against it; batch benchmark stub
- ✅ World state (`world/`): `WorldBook` (NPC roster) and `WorldState` (story flags, NPC fates, ending locks) implementing `WorldView` and `WorldSink`; `Game` now delegates world reads and writes to it; lifecycle, property and through-`Game` tests, and a benchmark stub
- ✅ Dialogue runner (`dialogue/`): `DialogueBook` (validated topic graph) and `Conversation` (start topics, follow-ups, conditions, locked checks, atomic effects, endings) over any `State`; `start` and `ends` added to the dialogue schema; hand-authored Doc Mitchell and Sunny Smiles graphs in `data/dialogue/slice_goodsprings.json`; 24 tests including a property test over random graphs, seven golden scenarios in `tests/scenarios/goodsprings_dialogue.json` and a menu benchmark stub (about 300 ns per menu)
- ✅ Goodsprings quest slice: hand-authored stage rewards and rule gates (`data/quests/slice_goodsprings.json`) merged by `normalize.py`; `Game` applies `on_enter` effects and fires consequence rules, undoes a failed change, and postpones rewards for unbuilt features into `Game::deferred()`; ten golden scenarios in `tests/scenarios/`
- ✅ Combat core (`combat/`): `Combat` runs a turn-based fight (action points, rounds, a win check) over a small `Combatant` trait, with the curated damage pipeline (weapon damage, resistance capped at 85, threshold with a 20% floor), bounded combat numbers, a swappable `HitModel` with a flat stand-in, and deterministic `Dice`; 29 tests including property tests and twelve golden scenarios in `tests/scenarios/combat.json`, and a fight benchmark stub (about 490 ns)
- ✅ TUI v0 (`crates/tui/`): an Elm-style `App` with a persistent layout (status line, scene, log, active-quest panel, key footer), an exploration screen and a dialogue screen over the engine's `Conversation`; property and headless render tests over the real Goodsprings data
- ✅ Character and inventory (`character/`, `inventory/`): `Character` (S.P.E.C.I.A.L. and XP, with level, hit points, action points, carry weight and starting skills derived from the curated formulas) and `Inventory` (item stacks, whole-number caps, weapon/outfit/head slots) over a 922-item `ItemBook`; `Game` owns both, so XP, caps and item rewards now apply instead of being deferred; the status line shows level and caps; the formulas, constants and XP table are pinned to `formulas.json` and `levels.json` by tests
- ✅ Combat wired into the game (`game/battle.rs`, `combat/ai.rs`, `crates/tui/src/combat/`): `Arsenal` checks every weapon, armor piece and encounter once at load; `Fighter` builds a `Combatant` from the character, the equipped weapon and armor, or from an NPC record; `Game` keeps the player's wounds between fights, offers encounters by quest state, starts a `Battle` and settles it (a win applies the encounter's effects once; a loss leaves the player down); the enemy side plays a plain tactic; the TUI has a combat screen and a game-over screen. The Ghost Town Gunfight is hand-authored in `data/combat/encounters.json`; 20 new tests including property tests over random fights
- ✅ Goodsprings gunfight path (dialogue): Ringo offers the quest (stage 5) and starts the countdown ("Let's do this", stage 60), Sunny joins the line (stage 65), and the fight is offered only from stage 65; the By a Campfire on the Trail quest starts from Sunny (stage 10) and ends with her errand to Trudy (stage 40); nine golden scenarios. The optional stages 20 to 50 are skipped for now
- ✅ Trudy's dialogue (`data/dialogue/slice_goodsprings.json`): the Cobb argument and the Ringo follow-ups, the news chains, the robot talk and the Speech 25 / Sneak 25 enlistment (flag `trudy_joined_militia`, open from stage 10 to 60); her radio and shop topics wait for the radio quest and vendors; eight golden scenarios, which can now set attributes
- ✅ Ammunition and reload: weapons hold a clip and spend rounds with every shot; a Reload action refills it from the rounds in the pack, foes reload when empty, the rounds fired leave the pack when the fight is settled, and Doc's starting kit includes 30 rounds of 9mm

- ✅ Leveling, perks and traits (engine): skill points at each level-up (`10 + 0.5 * intelligence`, half points carried over) and spending them, three tagged skills (+15), a perk pick every second level, up to two traits, and `Character::create`; `PerkBook` checks who may take which perk; a typed `Modifier` vocabulary (`shared/modifier.rs`) lets `data/perks/modifiers.json` make a perk or trait change a number, with five perks and two traits modeled so far; `Game::spend_skill_point` and `Game::choose_perk`; progression, property, real-data and contract tests
- ✅ Chet's dialogue (`data/dialogue/slice_goodsprings.json`): the Khans, New Vegas and ammo chatter, the gunfight supply talk (1000 caps or Barter 25; flag `chet_supplied_militia`) and the Powder Ganger shakedown (Barter 25 or Speech 25; flag `chet_shaken_down`, waits for Joe Cobb's quest start); his shop topics wait for vendors; eight golden scenarios
- ✅ Level-up screen (`crates/tui/src/character/`): a "Character" row in the scene (it says when skill points or perk picks are waiting) opens a screen to spend skill points on any of the 13 skills and to take an open perk, with the engine's own reason shown on a refusal and a note when the game does not model a perk yet; the real game now builds its `PerkBook`; long lists scroll with the cursor
- ✅ Doc Mitchell's paid and supply topics (`data/dialogue/slice_goodsprings.json`): healing at 100, 75 or 50 caps by how hurt the player is, the Medicine 25 lie that sets the flag Cobb's hand-over reads, and two doctor's bags once; the shared vocabulary gained `Condition::Health` and `Effect::Heal {}` (with `HealthPercent`, schema entries and contract tests), and `Game` answers them from its wounds; 17 golden scenarios
- ✅ Easy Pete's and Joe Cobb's dialogue (`data/dialogue/slice_goodsprings.json`): Pete's chatter and the Explosives 25 dynamite talk (flag `easy_pete_joined_militia`, open from stage 10 to 60); Cobb's pitch and deal that start Run Goodsprings Run at stage 10, the Ringo-death report (15 to 20), the supplies hand-over (45, waits for Doc's flag `doc_mitchell_gave_cobb_supplies`), the takeover call (60), and his chatter; 29 golden scenarios, and the scenario runner now builds the game with the NPC roster
- ✅ Character creation screen (`crates/tui/src/character/creation.rs`): before the first scene the player spends the 5 extra S.P.E.C.I.A.L. points, tags three skills and picks up to two traits, each in its own panel (Left/Right or Tab to move, Enter to open, Esc to close), with a Derived box and a Done button that stays locked until the choices are valid; a property test shows no key sequence breaks a limit or builds a character the engine would refuse
- ✅ Vendors and barter (`trade/`): `VendorBook` and `Market` hold each merchant's stock and caps, which change with every trade; `Game::buy` and `Game::sell` move caps and items between the player and a vendor as one unit (a dead or hostile vendor does not trade, and an equipped item cannot be sold); prices come from two curated Barter formulas (155 to 110 percent to buy, 45 to 90 percent to sell, from a secondary source because the wiki page could not be fetched), with the line rounded once; Chet, Trudy and Doc Mitchell are authored in `data/vendors/vendors.json` (placeholder stock); a dialogue topic can open a shop, and Doc's "I need medical supplies" does; 26 trade tests including a conservation property test, and a benchmark stub (about 2 microseconds per buy-and-sell pair)
- ✅ Trade screen (`crates/tui/src/trade/`): the vendor's stock beside the player's pack in two panels with a cursor each; Enter trades a stack of one or asks how many for a bigger stack (a bounded `Quantity`); the scene lists the shops in town and a dialogue topic with a `shop` opens the same screen, returning to the conversation afterwards; refusals reach the log in the engine's words; the app now builds the vendors; a property test shows no key sequence creates or destroys caps or items
- ✅ Pack screen and item use (`crates/tui/src/inventory/`, `Game::use_item`): a "Pack" row in the scene opens every stack with Use, Equip or Unequip, and Drop (with a "how many" question for a stack); `Game::use_item` heals and spends the item, keeps it at full health, and refuses an item whose effect is not modeled; `Game::drop_item` refuses the last equipped copy. What an item does when used is hand-authored in `data/items/uses.json` and merged into each consumable as `on_use` (stimpak 30, banana yucca fruit 7, bitter drink 36, purified water 10); a per-second effect is entered as its total until there is a game clock
- ✅ Game clock (`clock/`, `Game::wait`): `GameClock` counts minutes from day 1, 08:00 and `Span` is a non-zero length of time; time only moves forward and an overflow is refused; the scene offers "Wait an hour" and "Wait 8 hours", and the status line shows the day and time. Conditions and effects cannot mention time yet; property tests and a benchmark stub (about 110 ns per advance-and-format)
- ✅ Beds and sleep (`rest/`, `Game::sleep`): `BedBook` lists the beds, each at a place, and `restored` turns a sleep into hit points (linear, rounded up, eight hours restores everything; the length is a stand-in because the wiki gives none). Sleeping needs a bed, passes the time and closes wounds; waiting still heals nothing, so stimpaks and Doc Mitchell keep their value. Beds are hand-authored in `data/beds/beds.json` (one placeholder, Doc Mitchell's house); the scene shows "Sleep in ..." where a bed stands and now scrolls when it lists more rows than fit
- ✅ Reputation and karma (`reputation/`): `ReputationBook` checks the 13 factions and towns that carry a fame/infamy model (tier thresholds and the 4x4 title grid) once at load; `Standing` holds fame and infamy points per faction and one bounded karma number (-1000 to 1000; good from 250, evil from -250, a stand-in until the karma page is imported) and implements `StandingView` and `StandingSink`, so `Game` no longer refuses reputation and karma effects. A gain only adds and each score stops at 10,000; forcing a standing replaces both scores; an unmodeled faction, a negative or non-finite amount is refused and changes nothing. Rules can ask in two new ways beside the seven standings: `reputation_tier` ("infamy of tier 3 or more") and `reputation_points` ("a fame score of at least 40"), in the shared vocabulary, `common.schema.json` and the contract tests. The Ghost Town Gunfight gate now reads the wiki's rule exactly (infamy tier 3) and its last stage pays a placeholder 3 Goodsprings fame; property tests for the laws, four new golden scenarios and an extended one, and a benchmark stub (about 390 ns per gain-and-read round)
- ✅ Reputation consumers (`reputation/`, `trade/`, `crates/tui/src/reputation/`): a reputation screen (karma and, for each modeled faction, the title and the fame and infamy points); vendor discounts as data (`discount` with a percent and conditions), so Chet and Trudy give 25% to a player the town finds Accepted or better, or Mixed (the percentage is the roadmap's figure, a stand-in); their shop dialogue topics, shown only to such a player because the transcripts hold only the positive-reputation lines; the trade screen says when a cut is in force
- ✅ Travel and the quest journal (`travel/`, `Game::travel`, `crates/tui/src/travel/`, `crates/tui/src/quests/journal.rs`): `TravelBook` over the derived map links (two-way, checked at load), the player's place held by `Game`, a walk that moves the player and the clock together (two minutes a map pixel, rounded up; a stand-in), a travel screen with the walk times, and arriving looks up the people, shops and beds of the new place; a journal screen with active, done and failed quests
- ✅ Timed item effects (`effects/`, `ItemUse::Boost`): `ActiveEffects` holds each boost with the minute it ends and `Game::wait` (so sleep and travel too) ends them; `Character` reads the boosts for attributes, skills, maximum hit points, action points and carry weight, a chem's resistance adds to the armor's in a fight, and a dose replaces the last dose of the same item. A boost to maximum hit points is a loan: the wounds stay, so the extra hit points go with it, and the player is never killed by the repayment. Twelve items are usable (buffout, Med-X, mentats, party time mentats, jet, ant nectar, ant queen pheromones, healing powder, four sausages and pastes); heals over time are entered as their total because the clock counts minutes; addiction is not modeled
- ✅ Foes that depend on the story and the Run Goodsprings Run fights (`Foe.when`, `data/combat/encounters.json`): an enemy can be absent while conditions hold, so a helper who joined the town thins the Ghost Town Gunfight (six attackers as the wiki says if nobody helped, three if the town is fully prepared; which helper removes which ganger is a stand-in) while the game has no allies; "Kill Ringo" (stage 10 to 15) and "Attack the Goodsprings militia" (stage 60 to 70, which kills Sunny, Easy Pete and Trudy) finish Run Goodsprings Run, with placeholder loadouts, hit points and reputation

- ✅ Weapon realism in combat (`combat/hit.rs`, `combat/condition.rs`, `game/battle.rs`): the hit chance now comes from the shooter's skill with the weapon and the weapon's spread (`AimedHitChance`: 40 + skill / 2 - 3 * spread, kept between 5 and 95; a stand-in curve, since the wiki gives no formula), each weapon has its own reload cost (a reload of N seconds costs as much as N shots, never more than a whole turn), and weapons wear: every shot costs a point of condition, the damage multiplier falls from full to half as condition goes, and the wear is kept per weapon between fights (`Game::weapon_condition`). Property tests for the chance (skill never hurts, spread never helps, always between 5 and 95), for condition (losing points never helps; wearing in steps equals wearing at once) and for every fighter being able to pay for a reload
- ✅ Save and load (`saves/`, `Game::save`, `Game::restore`, "Save game" and "Load game" in the scene): a versioned JSON save of everything that changes in play (quests, world, standing, character, pack, shops, clock, place, wounds, boosts, weapon wear and the history of fights and rules). Loading starts from a game built from the data and puts each part back through the checks play uses, so a save that disagrees with the data is refused with the part named (a perk the game lacks, a stage that does not exist, wounds past the maximum, and so on) and a refusal changes nothing; writes go to a temporary file and are renamed over the save, so a crash leaves the old save or the new one. A round-trip property test over random play (the loaded game equals the saved one and goes on the same way), 29 refusal tests, version and file tests, and a benchmark stub (about 22 microseconds per save, write, read and load)
- ✅ Repair, crafting and gathering (`repair/`, `craft/`, `gather/`, `crates/tui/src/craft/`, `crates/tui/src/repair/`): a weapon repair kit lifts a worn weapon to the Repair skill's share of its condition and merging two copies adds a bonus on top (both capped by the skill, never worse), and Chet mends a weapon for caps up to his own Repair 50 (the rates, the 10% merge bonus, half the weapon's value for a full repair and Chet's service are stand-ins: the wiki gives none). The two copies of a weapon share one wear record, so a merge treats them as equally worn. Crafting sorts the 168 recipes into 84 the game can make (with stations, skill and ingredients checked, and `on_craft` reactions) and a stated reason for every other; stations and the two Goodsprings plants are hand-authored placeholders; crafting components are now carried. Making healing powder at the campfire moves By a Campfire on the Trail to stage 30. Run Goodsprings Run's optional stages 30 (Chet) and 40 (Doc) are set by the dialogue in either order. The save is version 2 and carries what was picked up.
- ✅ Weapon instances (`inventory/copies.rs`, `Game::{equip_copy, drop_copy, sell_copy}`): every weapon is its own object with an id and its own wear, so a spare you buy is new while the one in hand stays worn, only the weapon fought with wears, and merging names the two copies. The pack, trade and repair screens list one row per weapon with its condition. The save is version 3 and a version 2 save is upgraded on reading
- ✅ Vendor restocking (`trade/`): every vendor goes back to its starting stock and caps on every multiple of its interval (`restock_days`, 3 by default, a stand-in) since the game began, checked whenever the clock moves, so waiting in steps or at once gives the same shops
- ✅ Initiative and fleeing (`combat/`, `game/battle.rs`): turn order by Sequence (twice Perception, a stand-in), foes that are quicker shoot first (`Battle::open`), and "Run away" is one roll against `25 + 6 * Agility` kept between 5 and 95 (a stand-in); a fled fight keeps the wounds, rounds and wear and stays on offer
- ✅ Named saves and autosave (`saves/folder.rs`, `crates/tui/src/saves/`): a folder of named saves with a list showing day, time, place and experience, a typed name, a question before writing over, and an automatic save after a walk, a sleep and a settled fight
- ✅ Conditional perk and trait effects (`shared/modifier.rs`, `character/edge.rs`, `combat/`, `game/battle.rs`): a modifier can now name the weapon skill, the kind of foe or the item it applies to. Eleven new kinds (damage threshold and resistance, threshold ignored, damage, critical chance and critical damage, action point and reload costs, spread, weapon wear, healing) are summed by `Character::edge` for the weapon in hand and read when a `Fighter` is built, by `Combat` for the bonus against a foe's kind, by `Game::finish_battle` for wear and by `Game::use_item` for healing. Ten perks (Toughness, Finesse, Better Criticals, Math Wrath, Rapid Reload, Piercing Strike, Fast Metabolism, Demolition Expert, Entomologist, Hunter) and five traits (Built to Destroy, Kamikaze, Heavy Handed, Fast Shot, Trigger Discipline) use them; the wiki text of each is pinned by a test. Foes carry an optional `kind`. Two-way schema tests, property tests for the sums, the scoping and the threshold, and a benchmark stub (about 17 ns per edge read)
- ✅ Healing by Medicine (`ItemUse::MedicineHeal`, `Game::use_item`): the real game has no passive recovery, so waiting and walking still heal nothing; a stimpak restores 30 hit points plus one percent for every two points of Medicine (a stand-in: the wiki says Medicine changes how well stimpaks work and gives no formula), and Fast Metabolism adds 20 percent
- ✅ Taking things apart and tools that stay (`craft/`, `Ingredient.kept`): a breakdown recipe spends the item it names and gives the listed parts at the reloading bench (twelve of the sixteen; four name no item to take apart), a round taken apart never gives back more than making it used (tested over the real data), and a tool the wiki marks "not expended" is carried and kept. The craft screen shows both
- ✅ Save management (`saves/folder.rs`, `crates/tui/src/saves/`): delete (`d`) and rename (`r`) on the saves screen, each after a question and a rename never over another save; the automatic save keeps the one before it as "Previous autosave"; the old single `save.json` is listed as "Old save" for loading
- ✅ Save history (`saves/folder.rs`): the automatic save keeps five earlier ones (`autosave_previous`, `autosave_previous_2` to `_5`; five is a stand-in), each moved one step back as a new one is written and the oldest dropped, never over a damaged latest one; the names are reserved; opening the saves screen moves the old single `save.json` into the folder as the named save "Old save" (refused, and left where it is, if that name is taken)
- ✅ Three more breakdowns (`scripts/normalize.py`): .357 Magnum, .44 Magnum and the 20 gauge shell resolve to their one plain round, so 15 of the 16 breakdowns can be done; only the 12 gauge shell names no one round (twelve gauge has buckshot, slug and other loads, and picking one would be a guess)
- ✅ Radiation (`radiation/`, `Game::{rads, radiation_resistance, with_rads}`): `Rads` is a bounded 0 to 999 (the wiki's death at 1000 is not modeled); what comes in is cut by radiation resistance (the curated `2 * (endurance - 1)` percent plus Rad-X, kept between 0 and 100), what goes out is not; every 200 rads is a tier of sickness that costs one Endurance and one Agility (a stand-in; the wiki's table was not imported). Radiation is held in the save (version 4) and shown in the status line
- ✅ Addiction (`addiction/`, `Game::use_item_rolling`): `AddictionBook` reads each chem's risk from the data (38 consumables carry an addiction, 32 of them with a chance; the rest roll nothing); a dose rolls the dice, a roll at or below the chance hooks the player unless they already are (then only the last dose moves); withdrawal costs one Strength and one Agility per addiction, and an addiction wears off after three days without a dose (both stand-ins). The pack screen rolls and says "You are now addicted to ..."; `Game::use_item` never rolls
- ✅ Item use beyond boosts (`data/items/uses.json`, `ItemUse::Boost.rads`): 78 more consumables are usable, so 94 of 195 now are: 75 meals and drinks that heal, carry radiation or both (a heal over time is entered as its total; hardcore-only effects are left out), RadAway (-50 rads, kept by a player with none), Rad-X (+25 radiation resistance for 4 minutes) and the super stimpak (60 hit points, then -1 Strength and Agility for 2 minutes). The Doctor's bag is not usable: its wiki text is only about limb condition, and limbs are not modeled. A test pins each item's radiation to the wiki's text
- ✅ Doc Mitchell's cures (`data/dialogue/slice_goodsprings.json`): "Can you heal my radiation?" costs 100 caps (the transcript's figure) and takes every rad away; "I think I might be addicted" ends every addiction (the transcript names no price, so it is free). The shared vocabulary gained `Condition::Radiation`, `Condition::Addicted` and `Effect::Cure` (with `Ailment`), in the schema and the contract tests; seven golden scenarios, which can now start irradiated or addicted
- ✅ Allies in combat (`combat/ai.rs`, `combat/fight.rs`, `game/battle.rs`, `data/combat/encounters.json`): an encounter lists `allies` with the same shape as a foe (present while their `when` conditions hold), and they play themselves with the plain tactic, after the player and before the foes in turn order; `Combat::with_lead` names the player, whose fall loses the fight even while an ally stands, and `computer_turns` plays everyone else. In the Ghost Town Gunfight Sunny always fights beside the player, Easy Pete and Trudy join when they joined the militia, and the foes are the wiki's six whoever helped (Chet's supplies still keep one ganger away, a stand-in). A fallen ally is not killed in the world; the combat screen lists the allies on one line, and its actions scroll
- ✅ Poison and weapon mods (`poison/`, `weapon_mods/`, `game/state/fit.rs`, `data/engine/weapon_mods.json`): `Poison` holds a bounded number of points (999 at most), cut on the way in by the player's poison resistance (`5 * (endurance - 1)` plus chems, the curated formula) and paid out as wounds at 5 a minute by `Game::wait` (a stand-in rate; poison alone never takes the last hit point), shown on the status line; five consumables are now usable (antivenom, datura antivenom, fiery purgative, blood shield, bleak venom) through `cure_poison`, `poison` and a `poison_resistance` boost stat. `WeaponModBook` reads once, at load, which weapons each of the 111 mods fits and the numbers it changes (59 mods have effects `normalize.py` could read: damage, condition points and percent, magazine, spread; the rest, such as silencers and scopes, fit and change nothing); `Game::{fit_mod, remove_mod, mods_for, fitted_mods, fitting}` move a mod between the pack and one weapon copy, and the fight, the magazine and the condition when new all read what is fitted. The pack screen offers "Fit a mod" and "Take a mod off" on a weapon and lists what is fitted. The save is now version 5 (it gained poison and each weapon's mods; an older save is upgraded with none); property tests for the poison laws, real-data tests for the mods and game tests for items, time and antidotes

- ✅ Time of day (`shared/time.rs`, `Condition::TimeOfDay`, `WorldView::minute_of_day`): `MinuteOfDay` is a bounded minute of the day written `"HH:MM"` in data, and `TimeWindow` is a start (included) and an end (excluded) that may cross midnight; a window whose two ends are the same minute is refused, because it could mean none of the day or all of it (JSON Schema cannot compare two fields, so only Rust refuses it, and a test says so). `Game` answers from the clock. Property tests: a window and its swap split the day, the start is inside and the end is out. The `Effect::Wait` and a days-passed condition were left out: no rule needs them
- ✅ Conditional perks and traits (`shared/modifier.rs`, `character/situation.rs`, `character/sheet.rs`, `Game::settle`): a perk or trait record may list `conditional` gates, each a `when` list and the modifiers it switches on. A gate may ask only about health and the time of day (joined by `all`, `any`, `not`), and may not change maximum hit points, nor (under a health condition) Endurance, so a rule cannot contradict itself; both refusals happen when the data is read. `Modifier` gained `special` and `all_special` (attributes, kept inside 1 to 10). `Character` keeps the plain sum and the gates apart and re-sums when `Game` hands it a new `Situation`, which `Game` does whenever wounds, the clock or the character change (a living player is never left down by a falling maximum). Modeled: Nerd Rage and Night Person (base game), Hot Blooded and Early Bird (Old World Blues, so not offered yet). Property test: where a character stands depends on the situation it is in and not on the path to it
- ✅ Goodsprings defenders (`data/dialogue/slice_goodsprings.json`, `data/combat/encounters.json`): Victor can be asked for help (flag `victor_joined_militia`, open from stage 10 to 60) but never fights, because the transcript has him "doze off" through an override command; his "you didn't show up" and "the fight's over" topics open after the gunfight, for a recruit only. The two Goodsprings settlers that Trudy's Speech or Sneak 25 check enlists join the line with the same flag (a stand-in loadout: a 9mm pistol and 25 hit points; the wiki gives no count). Seven golden scenarios
- ✅ More consumables usable (`data/items/uses.json`): 61 more meals, drinks and chems (alcohol, Nuka-Cola, irradiated food, the Sierra Madre martini and others), so 160 of 195 now are.
